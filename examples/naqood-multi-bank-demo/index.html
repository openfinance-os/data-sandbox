Naqood-style multi-bank reconciliation

A worked example: a TPP-side accounting system fetches an SME persona's bank feeds across multiple LFIs in parallel via the Open Finance Data Sandbox, and reconciles them by IBAN identity. This is the Naqood ↔ Woven ↔ ADCB use case: same legal entity, multiple banks, one consolidated ledger.

Each persona declares a multi_lfi_footprint with primary / secondary / tertiary slots. The sandbox emits a separate v2.1-shaped bundle per slot at /fixtures/v1/bundles/<persona>/<slot>/<lfi>/seed-N/. The cross-bundle deterministic IBAN means the primary's self-to-<slot> beneficiary points at the same IBAN that the role bundle declares as its own account — so the TPP can prove "these are the same persona at two banks" without any out-of-band identifier.
